# How to replay a run in the 8-bit theatre

Replay a finished run's transcript as 8-bit characters around a table, a page at a time.

The theatre only reads what the run recorded: every line is a real message, in order, and every movement comes from a recorded event.

## Prerequisites

- Matrix Studio open and signed in, in the browser you will open the theatre from (the new tab reuses that session).
- A run that is no longer generating and has at least one message. The theatre has no live mode.

## Steps

1. Open the run.
2. Tap the space-invader icon in the header ("Replay in the 8-bit theatre"), or tap **⋯** and choose **Replay in the 8-bit theatre ↗**.

   Both are greyed out while the run is still going, and the menu entry says why.

3. In the new tab (`theatre.html?run=<run id>`), tap **▶ Start**.
4. Advance one page at a time: tap the dialogue box, or press Space or Enter. A press while a page is still typing finishes it.
5. Use the controls under the stage as needed:

   | Control | Does |
   |---|---|
   | ↺ | restart from the beginning |
   | ◀ and ▶▶ (or the left and right arrow keys) | previous and next page |
   | ▶ / ❚❚ (or P) | play and pause automatically |
   | 1×, 2×, 4× | change the speed |
   | ♫ | sound on or off |
   | the slider | jump to any line |

6. At the end, tap **↻ Replay** to watch again.

## What you will see

- Consultants walk in to answer, and a "?" marks the persona who asked.
- A scheduled message arrives by messenger.
- Working assumptions go up on the whiteboard under **On the board**, with **NEW** on a recent one.
- **SHIFT** on a speaker's name marks a recorded change of position; **SOURCES** means retrieved passages were in that message's prompt.
- A run that researched opens with the cast walking in from the library.
- A branch opens at its fork. Its title card says where it forked; scrub back with the slider for the lines it inherited.

## Check it worked

The title card names the run, its topic and its number of personas and lines, and the position readout under the stage shows "LINE n/N · TURN t" as you advance.
