# How to add consultants to a run

Give a run experts who are not in the conversation: personas can ask them questions mid-run, and they answer only from their own sources, with citations, or say the answer is not in their sources.

A consultant never takes a turn and holds no position.

## Prerequisites

- Matrix Studio open and signed in.
- Sources for each consultant: a knowledge collection ([How to manage knowledge collections](manage-knowledge-collections.md)), text you can paste, or web research ([How to research a subject before a run](research-before-a-run.md)). A consultant with no sources can only say that the answer is not in them.

## Steps

1. In the new-run wizard, go to the **Cast** step and scroll to **Consultants**.
2. Tap **+ Add consultant** (up to five).
3. Enter a **Consultant name** and their **Expertise**, for example "Rosa" and "local fire-safety rules for public buildings". The name must differ from every persona's and every other consultant's.
4. Give the consultant sources, in any combination:
   - tick one or more collections in the consultant's card;
   - open **Paste a source document** and paste a title and text;
   - on the **Knowledge** step, with **Research the subject before starting** on, keep **Consultant research — a library for each consultant** ticked.
5. Set **Consultations allowed per run** (0 to 20; 6 by default). Each consultation is a model call.
6. Launch the run.

## Check it worked

- In the conversation, a consultant's answer is marked **consultant** and starts with who asked and the question ("Mina asked: …"). Later speakers can use it.
- On a finished run, open **Asides** and choose **A consultant (answers from its sources)** to ask one yourself (see [How to ask an aside](ask-an-aside.md)).
- In the 8-bit theatre the consultant walks in to answer.
