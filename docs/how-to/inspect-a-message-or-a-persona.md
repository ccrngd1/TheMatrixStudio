# How to open a message's context and a persona's dossier

See why a message was said and what its speaker was drawing on, and open a persona's dossier of convictions, memories and messages.

## Prerequisites

- Matrix Studio open and signed in, with a run open. Both work on live and finished runs.
- For the "why" trace and the memory stream: a run with cognition on (on by default in the new-run form, under **Cognition (introspectable engine)**). Without it those parts say they were not recorded.

## Open a message's context

1. In the conversation, tap the message, or its turn number (such as **#05**).

   **Message context** opens beside the conversation (over it on a phone).

2. Read the sections:
   - **Why them, now**: the moderator's reason for picking this speaker, when one was recorded.
   - **Came after** and **Followed by**: the neighbouring messages; tap one to jump to it.
   - **Assumptions in force** at that turn, and a **SHIFT** note if the speaker said their position moved.
   - **In front of them**: the document passages retrieved for this message, and which ones it cites.
   - The message's cost and tokens.
3. Under **In their words**, tap **Why did they say that?** to load the speaker's own rationale, the goal it served and the memories it drew on.
4. To go further:
   - **Open …'s dossier** opens the speaker's dossier.
   - **Branch from turn NN** opens the scrubber at that turn (finished runs only).

Tap a citation inside a message to open the passage it points to.

## Open a persona's dossier

1. Tap the persona's portrait anywhere it appears: beside one of their messages, in the row of faces above the conversation, on the room map, or in the cast list.

   The dossier opens with the persona's turns, stance and firmest position at the top.

2. Use the four tabs:
   - **Convictions**: where they ended (once the run is summarised), their positions with firmness, what formed each and what would move them, what they will not weigh, formative events, the persona and goals. An underlying concern stated plainly is shown under its position as **CONCERN**; on a run with hidden agendas it is shown only as hidden, and the summary reveals it after the run.
   - **Memory**: what they last said, their memory stream and reflections, the document passages they drew on, their background documents and the knowledge bases they searched.
   - **Threads**: open and resolved threads, and their relationships with the others.
   - **Why?**: every message they said, newest first; tap **why?** on one for its trace.
3. Close it to return to the run.

**Regenerate avatar** in the dossier's header draws a new portrait, which is a paid image call.

## Check it worked

The drawer or dossier names the speaker and the turn you opened. If the "why" trace reads "Not recorded: this run had cognition off", turn cognition on for the next run.
